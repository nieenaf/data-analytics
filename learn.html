Learn About Data Analytics

Build your understanding from basic concepts to workplace applications

Definition

Data Analytics is the process of inspecting, cleaning, transforming and interpreting data to discover useful information, patterns and trends. The resulting insights can be used to support decision making and solve problems.

Key Features

Data Analytics involves several activities that help convert raw data into useful insights.

Data Collection

Gather relevant information from appropriate sources such as business systems, surveys and digital platforms.

Data Cleaning

Identify missing, duplicated or incosistent information before analysis.

Data Visualition

Present information through charts, graphs and dashboards to make findings easier to understand.

Advantages and Limitations

Advantages Limitations / Challenges
Supports evidence-based desicion making. Data may be incomplete or inaccurate.
Can improve understanding of customers and operations. Privacy and security must be considered.
Can help organisations monitor performance. Large datasets can be complex to manage.

Common Data Analytics Process

  1. Define the problem or question.
  2. Collect relevant data.
  3. Clean and prepare the data.
  4. Analyse the data.
  5. Visualise and communicate the findings.
  6. Use the insights to support decisions.

Workplace Applications

  • Business performance analysis.
  • Customer behaviour analysis.
  • Financial and sales reporting.
  • Healthcare information analysis
  • Education performance monitoring.
  • Operational and workplace planning.
