Data Analytics in Action

Explore how data analysis can be applied in different areas.

🎓

Education

Educational instituitions can use data analytics to understand student performance, attendence and learning progress. This helps teachers identify areas that need improvement.

  • Student performance reports
  • Attendence tracking
  • Learning Progress
🏢

Business

Business can use data analytics to understand their sales, customers and overall performance. The information can help them make better business decisions.

  • Sales trend analysis
  • Customer behaviour
  • Business performance
🏥

Healthcare

Healthcare organisations can use data analytics to understand patient information and improve their services. It can also help hospitals with planning and managing resources.

  • Patient information analysis
  • Hospital performance
  • Resource planning

Example: Turning Data Into Insight

Data Analytics help business understand sales by looking at products, months, locations and customers.

Raw Data Analysis Possible Insight
Monthly sales figures Compare sales across months Identify periods of higher or lower sales
Product sales Compare product performance Identify popular products
Customer information Group customers by characteristics Understand customer patterns

Daily Life

Data Analytics is not limited to organisations. Digital services can generate and analyse information to understand usage patterns, improve recommendations and monitor performance.
